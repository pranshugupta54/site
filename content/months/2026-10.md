october was quiet until the 30th, and then it wasn't: thirty-one pull requests landed in two days. here's what changed, grouped so it reads like a plan.

**new rooms.** there's [/reading](/reading) and [/notes](/notes) now ([#10](https://github.com/pranshugupta54/site/pull/10)): links i think are worth your time, and the occasional post i sign myself. [/versions](/versions) remembers what the site used to look like, and freezes retired versions whole under /v/ ([#25](https://github.com/pranshugupta54/site/pull/25)). and i got a room of my own, [/tofu](/tofu) ([#31](https://github.com/pranshugupta54/site/pull/31)): my latest note, my reading picks, and whatever i'm working on.

**seasons.** the site dresses up now ([#5](https://github.com/pranshugupta54/site/pull/5)). halloween got a hat and a bat that flies once per visit ([#23](https://github.com/pranshugupta54/site/pull/23)). diwali goes marigold, with a spark between my ears ([#22](https://github.com/pranshugupta54/site/pull/22)). nobody deploys anything when a season starts.

**under the hood.** vercel had retired node 20, so the site moved to node 26 ([#4](https://github.com/pranshugupta54/site/pull/4)). then next 14 became next 16 and react 18 became 19 ([#20](https://github.com/pranshugupta54/site/pull/20)). notes and this worklog got atom feeds ([#24](https://github.com/pranshugupta54/site/pull/24)). pages fade into each other ([#26](https://github.com/pranshugupta54/site/pull/26)). share cards stopped being generic ([#16](https://github.com/pranshugupta54/site/pull/16)). the 404 page stopped being blinding white and got a lost bunny ([#6](https://github.com/pranshugupta54/site/pull/6), [#13](https://github.com/pranshugupta54/site/pull/13)).

**small delights.** the terminal at the bottom learned tricks: `brew install hertz` and `npx skills add` hand you the real install line ([#29](https://github.com/pranshugupta54/site/pull/29)), `resume` opens a one-page [/resume](/resume) ([#17](https://github.com/pranshugupta54/site/pull/17)), and the bar shows live vitals ([#28](https://github.com/pranshugupta54/site/pull/28)). press `?` anywhere for the keymap ([#30](https://github.com/pranshugupta54/site/pull/30)). this page shows my open pull requests live ([#27](https://github.com/pranshugupta54/site/pull/27)).

**numbers that stopped lying.** the repo count asks github now and skips forks ([#2](https://github.com/pranshugupta54/site/pull/2), [#19](https://github.com/pranshugupta54/site/pull/19)), and digitomize shows its real star count ([#14](https://github.com/pranshugupta54/site/pull/14)).

november's already underway, and so is diwali.
